What is Vortexia?

Vortexia (also called Vortexia Bot) is a free all-in-one Discord bot. It brings Lavalink music, moderation, tickets, economy and leveling, welcome tools, embed builders and utilities into one bot, controlled with slash commands.

Official website and old address

This site, vortexia.run.place, is the official website. It replaces the earlier vortexia.top address, so if you are looking for the Vortexia Discord bot you used to find there, you are in the right place.

How to add Vortexia to your server

  1. Click Add Bot and choose your server.
  2. Authorize the bot in Discord.
  3. Type /help to open the command center, or browse the full command list.

Vortexia vs. other “Vortex” bots

Vortexia is its own bot and is not related to the moderation bot Vortex, Vortexus or other similarly named bots. If you searched for “Vortexia Discord bot”, this is the official site with the correct invite link.

Support

Questions or bug reports? Join the Vortexia support server on Discord. Vortexia is not affiliated with Discord Inc.
