Members check their coins with /balance, claim rewards with /daily, earn more with /work and see where they stand with /rank and /eco-leaderboard. The /profile command shows a profile card.

Combine it with the music bot, tickets and welcome tools such as /welcome and /autorole so one bot covers your whole community.

How to get started

  1. Invite Vortexia to your Discord server.
  2. Tell members to type /daily and /work to start earning coins.
  3. Use /rank to see level and XP, and /eco-leaderboard to see the richest users.
  4. Set up /welcome and /autorole to greet and organise new members.

Economy & leveling bot FAQ

What economy commands does Vortexia have?

/balance, /daily, /work, /rank and /eco-leaderboard.

How do members check their level?

Members run /rank to see their level and XP.

Can Vortexia welcome new members?

Yes. Use /welcome for the welcome panel and /autorole to give a role on join.
