What makes the best free Discord bot?

Most servers end up running five bots for music, moderation, tickets, levels and welcome messages. An all-in-one bot replaces them with one invite, one permission set and one command style.

What to look for

  • Music: a stable player with queue, filters and 24/7 mode — see Vortexia music.
  • Moderation: ban, kick, timeout, warn, purge and AutoMod — see Vortexia moderation.
  • Tickets: panels with claim and close — see Vortexia tickets.
  • Economy & leveling: coins, XP and leaderboards — see Vortexia economy.
  • Free to use: no features locked behind premium.

Vortexia covers all of the above under modern slash commands. Add it to your server or browse the command list first.
