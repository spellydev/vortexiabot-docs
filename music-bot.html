A free Lavalink-powered music player for your Discord voice channels, controlled from one modern panel.

Music that lives in one panel

Vortexia plays music through Lavalink, a dedicated audio server used by many Discord music bots. Instead of memorising dozens of commands, you open the player with /music and control everything from the panel: play, queue, filters, lyrics and 24/7 mode.

Music is only one part of Vortexia — the same bot also handles moderation, tickets and economy and leveling.

How to get started

  1. Invite Vortexia to your Discord server.
  2. Join a voice channel.
  3. Type /music to open the player panel.
  4. Use Play on the panel to search or paste a song, then manage the queue, filters and lyrics from the same panel.

Music bot FAQ

How do I play music with Vortexia?

Join a voice channel, run /music and use the Play button on the player panel.

Does Vortexia support filters and lyrics?

Yes. The Lavalink music panel includes queue controls, audio filters, lyrics and a 24/7 mode.

Is the Vortexia music bot free?

Yes. Vortexia is free to invite and use.
