FAQ

Vortexia Bot — frequently asked questions

What is Vortexia Discord Bot?

Vortexia (also written Vortexia Bot) is a free all-in-one Discord bot with music, moderation, tickets, economy, leveling, welcome messages, embed builders and utility slash commands.

How do I add Vortexia Bot to my Discord server?

Click the Add Bot button on vortexia.run.place, choose your server and authorize. Then type / in Discord to see all Vortexia slash commands.

Is Vortexia free?

Yes. Vortexia is free to invite and use on your Discord server.

Does Vortexia have a music player?

Yes. The /music command opens a Lavalink-powered player panel with queue, filters, lyrics and 24/7 mode.

What moderation commands does Vortexia have?

Ban, kick, timeout, warn, purge, lock, slowmode, plus an AutoMod panel and security tools.

Is vortexia.run.place the official Vortexia website?

Yes. vortexia.run.place is the official information and invite page for the Vortexia Discord bot. It is the successor of the older vortexia.top site.

Where can I get help with Vortexia?

Join the official Vortexia support server on Discord: https://discord.gg/7RfWAKeAZZ
