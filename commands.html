How Vortexia slash commands work

All Vortexia commands are Discord slash commands. Type / in any channel where the bot can see messages, pick a command and fill in the options. For music, join a voice channel first and open the player with /music. Moderation, ticket and security commands are designed for staff.

Looking for a specific feature? See the music bot, moderation bot, ticket bot and economy and leveling pages.
