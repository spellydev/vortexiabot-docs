Support tickets without the clutter

Create a ticket panel with /ticket setup. Members open a ticket from the panel, and staff can /claim it, add other people with /add, rename it with /rename and finish with /close.

Pair tickets with Vortexia moderation and the builder commands to run a complete support and community setup from a single bot.

How to get started

  1. Invite Vortexia to your Discord server.
  2. Run /ticket setup name:Support in the channel where you want the panel.
  3. When a member opens a ticket, staff use /claim to take it.
  4. Use /add or /rename if needed, and /close when the issue is solved.

Ticket bot FAQ

How do I create a ticket panel in Discord with Vortexia?

Run /ticket setup and give the panel a name, for example /ticket setup name:Support.

Which ticket commands are available?

/ticket setup, /claim, /close, /add and /rename.

Is the Vortexia ticket bot free?

Yes. Vortexia is free to invite and use.
