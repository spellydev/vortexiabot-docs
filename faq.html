Vortexia Discord bot FAQ

General

Is Vortexia free?

Yes. Vortexia is free to invite and use, with no premium paywall.

Is Vortexia safe?

Vortexia only uses the permissions you approve at invite time and never asks for your Discord password. Read the privacy policy.

Is this the official site?

Yes. vortexia.run.place is official and replaces the old vortexia.top address.

Music

How do I play music?

Join a voice channel, run /music and press Play in the panel. Details on the music bot page.

Music is not playing — what now?

Check that the bot can Connect and Speak in your voice channel, then try another search. Streaming sources can temporarily fail; report persistent issues in the support server.

Moderation & tickets

How do I set up AutoMod?

Open the AutoMod panel from /help and enable the rules you want. See the moderation bot page.

How do I create a ticket panel?

Run /ticket setup. See the ticket bot page.
