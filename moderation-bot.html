A complete moderation toolkit

Vortexia gives moderators the commands they use every day: /ban, /kick, /timeout, /warn with a warning history, /purge to clean up messages, plus /lock, /unlock and /slowmode to control busy channels.

For automatic protection, the /automod panel and /banword commands help you filter unwanted content, and /report lets members flag a user to your team.

How to get started

  1. Invite Vortexia to your Discord server.
  2. Type /automod to open the AutoMod panel and choose your protections.
  3. Add filtered words with /banword add.
  4. Use /warn, /timeout, /kick and /ban when moderators need to act, and check history with /warnings.

Moderation bot FAQ

What moderation commands does Vortexia have?

Ban, softban, unban, kick, timeout, unmute, warn, warnings, clearwarns, purge, lock, unlock and slowmode.

Does Vortexia have AutoMod?

Yes. Open the AutoMod panel with /automod and manage filtered words with /banword.

Is Vortexia a good moderation bot for small servers?

Vortexia is free and covers the common moderation tasks, so small and large servers can both use it.
